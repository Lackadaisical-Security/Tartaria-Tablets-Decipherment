## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Tartaria Tablets represent potentially the earliest writing system in Europe, predating Sumerian cuneiform by approximately 1,000 years. Discovered in 1961 at the Neolithic site of Tărtăria in Alba County, Romania, these three clay tablets feature symbols that have puzzled researchers for decades. The Universal Decipherment Methodology v2.0 will apply massive dataset correlation across 150+ scripts, with special emphasis on:

1. **Vinča symbols** (contemporary Balkan culture)

4. **Linear Elamite** (proto-writing evolution)
